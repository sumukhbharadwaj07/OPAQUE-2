Opaque

Needed for pages that are just a picture, such as a scanned document or an ID card, where there is no text in the page to read. Uses Florence-2.

Florence-2 finds photographs of people and QR codes in any picture it reads and covers them, without needing a rule.

Certain values (Aadhaar, cards, passwords, addresses) are always hidden. Uncertain ones — a name that might be a public figure, the city you live in — are hidden too, but shown to you first so you can reveal them.
